## Voice

Scientific, credible and trustworthy — a longevity-science nutraceutical brand. Confident about the research and the formulation, but disciplined and compliant in how benefits are stated (structure/function language, never disease claims). Speaks to informed adults who take healthy aging seriously: clear, evidence-forward, premium, calm. No hype, no miracle tone.

## No-gos (hard don'ts)

COMPLIANCE IS THE #1 RULE (US dietary supplement / Amazon). NEVER claim to cure, treat, prevent, reverse or diagnose any disease or aging itself. Do NOT state as literal fact that the product 'lengthens telomeres', 'activates/increases stem cells', 'regenerates cells', 'reverses aging', 'extends life/lifespan' or 'anti-aging' as a promised outcome — use hedged STRUCTURE/FUNCTION wording exactly like the live Amazon listings: 'designed to support', 'formulated to support telomere-related pathways', 'supports healthy aging', 'supports cellular health / longevity pathways'. No 'clinically proven', 'doctor-recommended', 'FDA-approved', 'guaranteed results', 'best/#1'. No disease names, no medical/scary imagery, no before/after, no lab-coat doctor implying medical treatment. No prices/URLs on-image (the 90-day guarantee badge is fine). The DTC site (lifecoderx.com) uses more aggressive claims (Stem Cell Activation, Regeneration, Life Extension) — DO NOT copy those onto Amazon creative.
