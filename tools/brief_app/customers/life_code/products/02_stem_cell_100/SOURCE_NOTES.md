## The one thing that makes this product different

Competitors sell "boost your stem cell count". Stem Cell 100® deliberately does **not**:

> "Each person only has a limited number of stem cells and each can divide only a limited
> number of times. As a result using them up faster may not be a good strategy."

The product works on the **effectiveness and longevity** of the stem cells a person already
has, **and on the micro-environment (the niche) around them**, which also degrades with age.

This is the whole positioning. It is a real, defensible, competitor-shaped argument — the
kind of claim a shopper can check — and it is the single most under-used asset the brand has.
Nobody else in this category is marketing the niche.

## The peer-reviewed lifespan study

Published in **PLoS One 2015, 10:e0119068** — a peer-reviewed journal. This is extremely rare
for a supplement and is the brand's strongest trust asset.

Design: 3 treated cages (T1–T3) and 3 untreated control cages (C1–C3), 500 Drosophila fruit
flies per cage (250 male, 250 female).

| Result | Figure |
|---|---|
| Median lifespan | **+123%** (more than doubled) |
| Longest-lived treated fly | 89 days vs 48 days control |
| Oldest 5% of treated group | lived **77% longer** than the oldest 5% of controls |
| Survival advantage | present at **all ages**, not only in old age |

**HOW THIS MAY BE USED:** as what it is — a published fruit-fly study. Always name the animal
and name the journal. It must never be phrased, framed or laid out so that a shopper could
read a human lifespan claim out of it. "Median lifespan more than doubled in fruit flies.
Peer-reviewed, PLoS One 2015" is honest. "Live twice as long" is not, and would be a serious
compliance failure as well as a lie.

# COMPLIANCE — READ BEFORE WRITING ANY CLAIM

Mandatory disclaimer wherever benefits are stated:

> These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. This product is not intended to
> diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

The client's own wording: *"Stem Cell 100® is not a preventive or treatment for any disease."*

### Never use, under any circumstances

- **Anything about cancer, tumours, or the breast, colon, pancreas or prostate.** References
  40–46 are in-vitro and animal oncology studies. They exist on the client's page; they must
  not reach Amazon in any form, including as a citation number, an organ name or an icon.
  This is the single fastest route to a listing takedown in this category.
- Any human lifespan or longevity outcome. The lifespan data is from fruit flies.
- "Cure", "treat", "prevent", "reverses aging", "anti-aging" as an outcome, or any named
  condition (diabetes, hypertension, high cholesterol, Alzheimer's, dementia).
- Blood glucose, pressure or cholesterol benefits **without** the "already in the normal
  range" qualifier.
- "Clinically proven", "doctor recommended", "FDA approved".

### Safe framing

- "Supports", "helps maintain", "formulated to support" — never "improves" or "restores"
- Name the study animal and the journal whenever the lifespan data appears
- Ingredient facts, amounts, capsule counts and dosing are safe: they are label data
- "Peer-reviewed", "published research", "standardised botanicals" are safe and accurate

# SCIENTIFIC PRECISION — the details this client will check

Life Code is run by a molecular biologist. Copy that is loosely true reads to that audience
as copy nobody checked, which is exactly the verdict the first plan got. Each of the
following was wrong in an earlier draft and has been corrected.

### L-theanine is not a botanical

The SC100 blend has **eight ingredients**, not eight botanicals:

- **seven plant extracts**, from **six species** — Astragalus membranaceus (root),
  Vaccinium uliginosum (fruit), pine bark OPCs, Schisandra chinensis (fruit),
  Drynaria (rhizome), and Polygonum multiflorum twice, as stem and as He Shou Wu root
- **one amino acid** — L-theanine

Write "eight ingredients" or name the split. Never "eight botanicals".

### Genistein is not a botanical either

TeloMax's TX100 blend is **five plants plus one isoflavone**. Genistein is a compound, not
a plant extract. Same rule.

### Two entries, one species

Polygonum multiflorum (stem) and He Shou Wu (root) are the same plant in two parts. Anyone
counting distinct species gets six, not eight. Say so on the tile rather than letting a
formulator find it.

### Use the oldest 5%, not the longest-lived fly

The study's headline comparisons are not equally strong:

| Statistic | Strength |
|---|---|
| Median lifespan +123% | Robust. Whole-group measure. **Lead with this.** |
| Oldest 5% lived 77% longer | Robust. Group comparison. **Use for the chart.** |
| Longest-lived 89 days vs 48 | **n = 1. Do not chart it.** |

The client's own page says of the single longest-lived fly: *"It is possible that [it] lived
longer for other reasons such as genetic mutation."* Charting an individual against an
individual, when the client has already hedged it in writing, is the fastest way to look
like we skimmed the study. The chart now compares the oldest 5% of each group.

### The study was published, not the formula

"Formula published in PLoS One" is wrong. A **study of the formula** was published.
Formulas are not published; papers are.

### "Faster acting" is not substantiated

The client's page calls Stem Cell 100+ "more powerful and faster acting". That is marketing
wording with nothing behind it, and on Amazon it is an unsubstantiated superiority claim.
Removed from the creative. The documented difference is factual and stronger anyway: Plus
adds **Rhodiola Rosea** and **Tulsi** to the SC100 blend.

### Always name the species with the lifespan number

Never "+123% lifespan" alone. Always "+123% median lifespan in Drosophila fruit flies", and
state that it is an animal result in the same breath. This is both the compliance
requirement and the reason the page is trustworthy.

### Open label question

The client's ingredient list gives **"Drynaria Rhizome (root)"**. A rhizome is a modified
stem, not a root. The creative says rhizome. Confirm which wording is on the printed
supplement facts panel and match it, since that panel is the legal document.
