## The positioning

Telomere health and cellular wellness, via Life Code's multi-pathway approach. Where Stem
Cell 100 argues about the stem cell niche, TeloMax is about the caps on the chromosomes.
The two are complementary and many customers take both, which is worth saying on the
creative rather than losing the shopper to a comparison tab.

## The formulator

*Dr. Bryant Villeponteau, Ph.D.* — the listing credits his **background in molecular biology
and longevity science**. The Stem Cell 100 listing puts it more strongly, as **decades of
research experience** in those fields. Both wordings are the client's own and may be used.

A more specific, checkable credential would be worth considerably more. Ask the client for
it in writing; do not source it from memory and do not infer it.

## NOT applicable to this product

**The PLoS One 2015 fruit-fly lifespan study was run on the SC100 formula.** It must not
appear on TeloMax in any form. Attaching a study to a formula it did not test is exactly the
thing that loses a science-led client's trust for good, and it is the failure this whole
round of work exists to correct.

4. Whether the "Multipath Regeneration (MuR)" name is still in use. It appeared in earlier
   copy as an internal approach name; it explains a process rather than answering a buyer,
   so it has been dropped from the Q&A.

## Compliance, specific to this product

Telomere wording must stay structure/function without exception:

- ✅ "supports telomere health", "telomere support"
- ✅ "telomeres are the protective caps on your chromosomes and shorten as cells divide" —
  textbook biology, stated about the body rather than about the product
- ❌ "lengthens telomeres", "restores telomere length", "activates telomerase",
  "reverses cellular aging"

The line between the two is whether the sentence describes biology or promises an outcome.
