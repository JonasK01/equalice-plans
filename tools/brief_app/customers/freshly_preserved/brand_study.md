## Voice

Clean, bright and elegant. Celebrates REAL edible flowers that keep their true color and natural shape long after fresh blooms would wilt — nothing artificial. Culinary and creative, premium but approachable, a little joyful ('Small Kitchens — Big Possibilities'). Speaks to bakers, pastry chefs, mixologists, caterers, event planners, food stylists, DIY/resin crafters and creative home hosts who want showpiece presentation without the hassle of fresh flowers.

## Photo rules

BRIGHT, CLEAN, AIRY, ELEGANT — the brand lives on freshness + beauty. White / soft daylight backgrounds, high-key lighting, generous negative space. Palette is the FLOWERS THEMSELVES: pansy purple + orchid pink + marigold orange + rose red + soft green, on white (client's stated brand colors). HERO the promise: the freeze-dried bloom holding its BRIGHT COLOR & NATURAL SHAPE, ideally beside a wilting fresh flower to prove 'shelf-stable, never spoils'. Show the payoff scenes buyers search for: a decorated cake / cupcake / wedding cake topper, a cocktail or mocktail garnish, a charcuterie / grazing board, plated fine-dining, resin/DIY craft. ALWAYS give a SIZE reference (on a fingertip, coin, cupcake) — 'smaller than expected' is the #1 review complaint. Defuse fragility: show whole, intact blooms + a protective/resealable jar or clamshell. Earn the click with on-pack BADGES buyers scan for: Real Edible Flowers (Not Artificial) · 100% Natural · Shelf-Stable · No Preservatives · No Added Sugar · Food-Grade · Made in USA · Whole Blooms + Petals. Product may be composited in but must look REAL and keep TRUE natural color (client OK'd photoshop 'as long as it looks real' — never over-saturate past the real bloom). English overlays (USA), PUNCHY (<=5 words). One idea, one badge, one prop per image.

## No-gos (hard don'ts)

No dull, dark or moody backgrounds — they fight the 'fresh & bright' identity (never a black background behind the logo). No fake / plastic / over-saturated color that misrepresents the real bloom (buyers already complain 'not as bright as pictures' — stay honest). Never imply the flowers are inedible decoration, or add preservatives / added sugar / artificial ingredients. No 'best / #1 / guaranteed', no health-cure claims, no prices or URLs on-image. No cheap clip-art or un-licensed stock.
