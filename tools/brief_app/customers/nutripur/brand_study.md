## Voice

Modern, science-trusted, parent-warm. Clinical credibility without being cold. Health Canada-anchored.

## Photo rules

Clean studio + real kid/parent moments (homework, breakfast, after-school). Kids in profile, from behind, or with face naturally obscured by activity (head-down on homework, hand near mouth) — soft brand signal, not face-forward portrait. Stock photos with model releases acceptable per brand brief. Soft natural light. Bottle visible in every lifestyle frame. Orange flavour visual cue (orange slices, capsules) supports format clarity. Health Canada + IFOS + Made in Canada badges where space allows. White / cream / soft teal mood backgrounds.

## No-gos (hard don'ts)

Cartoon kid mascots (looks cheap). Sugar/candy aesthetic — this is a clinical supplement, not a gummy. Overpromise visuals (trophies, A+ grades, "smartest in class"). Hospital / pharmacy sterile look — Nutripur is natural-clinical, not pharma-clinical. Posed staring-into-camera kid portraits. Anything implying medical diagnosis or cure. Cluttered countertops.
