## Key formulation facts (from nutripur.com/ca + retail listings)
- **Elemental magnesium: 100 mg per capsule** → **400 mg at the maximum 4-capsule daily dose.**
- **Form:** 100% fully chelated **Albion® Magnesium Bisglycinate Chelate TRAACS®**
  (The Real Amino Acid Chelate System) — patented true chelation, magnesium bound to
  two glycine amino acids.
- **Contains NO magnesium oxide** (key differentiator; reflected in the Amazon title).
- **No artificial additives or fillers.**
- **Non-medicinal ingredient:** Hypromellose (vegetarian capsule) — the ONLY NMI →
  vegan/vegetarian-friendly.
- **Count:** 180 vegetarian capsules (long supply; 45-day supply at 4 caps/day).
- **Positioning tagline (brand site):** "Superior absorption — up to 5x higher absorption.
  Gentle on the stomach."
- **No laxative side effects** (vs. cheaper magnesium forms / oxide).
