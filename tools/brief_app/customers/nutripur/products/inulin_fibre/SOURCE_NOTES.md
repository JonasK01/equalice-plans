## Full formula / amounts (verbatim from label)
Medicinal Ingredients — Each scoop contains:
- **Inulin (from chicory) (Cichorium Intybus) (root) — 5 g** — Halal & Kosher Certified
- **Bacillus coagulans (MTCC 5856) (Whole Cell) — 2 billion CFU** — Halal & Kosher Certified
Non-Medicinal Ingredient:
- GMO-free sweet potato maltodextrin (from the Lactospore® (Bacillus coagulans) preparation)

Trademarked ingredients:
- **Orafti®** (Inulin) — registered trademark of Beneo
- **Lactospore®** (Bacillus coagulans) — registered trademark of Sabinsa Corporation

## Dosing (verbatim)
"Recommended dose: Adults: Take 1 scoop, 1 to 3 times a day. Mix 1 scoop in a cup
(250 mL) of water, favorite beverage, coffee, tea or daily smoothie. Take 2 to 3 hours
before or after taking other medications, antibiotics or natural health products."

## Cautions / contraindications (label — for compliance awareness, not for copy)
- Consult a practitioner if laxative effect doesn't occur within 7 days, or for sudden bowel changes,
  rectal bleeding, nausea/fever/vomiting/bloody diarrhea/severe abdominal pain, diabetes, or anxiety/depression.
- Contraindication: do not use if immune-compromised (e.g. AIDS, lymphoma, long-term corticosteroids).
- Known adverse reactions: may cause temporary gas and/or bloating.
