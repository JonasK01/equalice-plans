- Helps relieve nervous tension, restlessness and nervousness.
- Helps relieve muscle, joint, and nerve pain, including pain associated with rheumatoid conditions

- Acts as a restorative tonic and supports energy metabolism.
- Helps improve memory.
- Acts as a hormone normaliser to help stabilise the menstrual cycle and relieve premenstrual and

- Helps in the maintenance of bones, cartilage, teeth, and gums.

## Dosing
- Adults: Take **1 to 2 capsules twice daily.**
- Suitable for prolonged use across perimenopause, menopause and post-menopause.

## Cautions / contraindications (FROM BOX)
- Consult a health care practitioner before use if you have a liver disorder or develop symptoms of
  one, if symptoms persist or worsen, or if you take hormone-containing medications (e.g. progesterone
  preparations, oral contraceptives) or hormone replacement therapy.
- May cause drowsiness — use caution when driving or operating heavy machinery.
- Do not use if pregnant, breastfeeding, or trying to conceive, except on professional advice.
- Keep at room temperature.
