- **Lutein 20 mg** (from LUTEMAX 2020 marigold / Tagetes erecta oleoresin)

- **Bilberry 4:1 extract 600 mg** (Vaccinium myrtillus fruit) = equivalent to **2400 mg** whole bilberry
- **Organic Canadian Blueberry 200 mg** (Vaccinium corymbosum fruit)
- **Vitamin A 1500 mcg RAE (5000 IU)** (as palmitate)
- Non-medicinal: sunflower oil, hypromellose (**vegetarian capsule**)
- Does NOT contain artificial colours/flavours/preservatives; vegan / gluten-free / lactose-free
- **60 vegetarian capsules** per bottle · Adults: **take 1 to 2 capsules daily** · 1–2 month supply
- GMP certified · Made in Canada by Nutripur
