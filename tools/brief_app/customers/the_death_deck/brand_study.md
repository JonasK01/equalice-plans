## Voice

Warm, playful and disarming. The brand takes the one subject everybody avoids and makes it feel like a good evening with people you love — never solemn, never spooky, never clinical. Created by a hospice social worker, so it has real authority, but it wears it lightly: curious rather than heavy, funny where funny is honest, and always on the player's side. It gives permission ('you can talk about this, and it's OK to laugh') instead of instructing. The reaction we want is: 'I could actually do this with my family.'

## No-gos (hard don'ts)

NO dark or gothic styling — no black backgrounds, no horror or Halloween cues, no cobwebs, no candles-in-the-dark. NO funeral imagery — no coffins, headstones, urns, graveyards, lilies or hearses. NO clinical or medical staging — no hospital beds, scrubs, IV lines, charts. NO overly sentimental treatment — no soft-focus grief, no crying, no memorial-slideshow mood. Nothing scary or depressing, full stop. The skull in the logo is a friendly graphic mark, never rendered realistically or menacingly. No 'best / #1 / guaranteed', no medical, grief-therapy or end-of-life-advice claims, no prices or URLs on-image. No cheap clip-art or un-licensed stock.
