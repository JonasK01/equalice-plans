# CLAIM RULES

### "Sterilization" against a 4-log result

Sterilization means complete destruction of microbial life; the accepted standard is 6-log.
A 4-log result is 99.99%, which is a **reduction** — disinfection, not sterilisation. An
infection-control lead or facilities manager reads that difference instantly.

All creative says **reduction**, paired with **third-party certified**, which is the stronger
line anyway. The product name and the partner name (Invictus Sterilization) stay exactly as
they are — those are names, not claims.

The same applies to "eliminate": the certified figure is a 99.99% reduction, not elimination.

### The verb is "reduces". Never "kills"

A complete claim needs three parts: **how much · of what · what happens to it.** Drop any one
and the sentence stops being a claim.

- ❌ `99.99% UNDER 60 MINUTES` — how much, but of what?
- ❌ `99.99% OF AIRBORNE PATHOGENS` — how much and of what, but what happens to them?
- ✅ `REDUCES 99.99% OF PATHOGENS` — complete
- ✅ `99.99% REDUCTION, CERTIFIED` — complete

**"Kills" is not available to us, and not because it is too strong.** A device claiming to
kill microorganisms is making a pesticidal claim under US law and needs an EPA registration
to carry it. Amazon enforces this. The same applies to "eliminates", "destroys" and
"sterilizes".

It is also simply inaccurate here: the certified result is a **4-log reduction**. That is a
reduction, not a kill.

Where a UV-specific verb is wanted, **"inactivates"** is both permitted and technically
correct — UV-C damages nucleic acid so the organism cannot replicate, which is inactivation
rather than killing.

### The 99.99% does not cover everything

The certificate covers **microbial** reduction: bacteria, viruses, mold, fungus.

It does **not** cover VOCs, gases, odors, dust, pollen or smoke — those are captured by
filtration, with no log figure behind them.

Any tile listing pollutants must keep the two groups visually separate, so the percentage
never reads as applying to all of them. Tile 02 does this with two arcs.

### SARS-CoV-2 and COVID-19

**Off every tile until the registration is confirmed.** Virus-kill claims for devices sit in
EPA territory in the US, and Amazon has removed listings in this category aggressively. With
the paperwork it becomes the strongest tile on the page; without it, it stays off.
